Dear Valued Guests and Travel Partners,

We're looking forward to welcoming you aboard the beautiful Norwegian Viva for your voyage departing August 10, 2026. As you prepare for your journey, and as part of our commitment to delivering a seamless, memorable experience, we'd like to share an update to your thoughtfully planned itinerary. Travel partners, please ensure any impacted guests have the opportunity to review the details below.

While every voyage is carefully designed, occasional adjustments allow us to deliver the smoothest and most enjoyable experience possible. Due to limited availability of essential port services in Kotor, Montenegro, we've made the decision to remove this call to ensure a more seamless day ashore and an overall better guest experience.

In its place, we're pleased to introduce Bar, Montenegro. Similar to Kotor, Bar offers a rich sense of history set against dramatic coastal scenery, with walkable areas, ancient ruins, a lively waterfront, and easy access to Montenegro's natural beauty, creating a relaxed and rewarding way to experience the Adriatic coast. Please see the updated itinerary below.
